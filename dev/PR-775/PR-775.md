# Implementation

(S11) from Dorheim et al. 2024 for our previous $RF_{strat_{H_2O}}$
equation, we had

$$ RF_{strat_{H_2O}} = 0.0485 \frac{[CH_4](t) - [CH_4]_0}{1831-[CH_4]_0}$$
which only accounts strat $H_2O$ created from methane oxidation with OH
radicals. However as part of the indirect climate effects of $H_2$ we
also have $H_2$ emissions contributing to $RF_{strat_{H_2O}}$. The new
implementation of $RF_{strat_{H_2O}}$ is now

$$ RF_{strat_{H_2O}} = 0.0485 \frac{[CH_4](t) - [CH_4]_0}{1831-[CH_4]_0} +  0.00019 (E_{H_2}(t) - {E_{H_2}}_0)$$
Where the coefficient of $0.00019$ W m −2 per Tg yr− 1 is the
multi-model mean from Sand et al. 2023.

## GWP100

Sand et al. report the AWGP 100 for H2 from various ESMs have included
results broken down by each of the three indirect $H_2$ effects so we
can use that to compare our $H_2$ implementation here.

$$\text{GWP}_{100} = \frac{\int_{0}^t \Delta \text{RF H}_2}{\int_{0}^t \Delta \text{RF CO}_2}$$
where the $\Delta \text{RF H}_2$ is from a pulse of 1 Tg of $\text{H}_2$
and $\Delta \text{RF CO}_2}$ is from a pulse of 1 Tg of $\text{CO}_2}$.
